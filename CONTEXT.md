# max-homepage

Max's personal/professional homepage: a portfolio site for clients, collaborators, and recruiters, with a Three.js landing hero, project showcase, and contact page.

## Language

**Homepage**:
The site's root page. Doubles as the Landing Hero — visitors land directly on the interactive bird scene, not a separate marketing page above it.
_Avoid_: Landing page (use only when specifically emphasizing the hero-scene aspect of the homepage)

**Landing Hero**:
The interactive Three.js scene of shy birds that reacts to mouse/touch position, serving as the homepage's hero. A permanent, evolving piece of the site rather than a throwaway demo.
_Avoid_: Bird demo, intro animation

**Figure**:
One of the animated 3D bird characters in the Landing Hero (e.g. `bird1`, `bird2`, `bird3`). Narrower than "any 3D model" — in this project, a Figure is specifically a bird.
_Avoid_: Model, character (when referring to the birds specifically, say Figure)
